How I Work

  • Get hands-on with the product rather than relying solely on specifications or SME explanations.
  • Work directly with engineers and product managers to resolve ambiguity before it reaches the documentation.
  • Write for the audience and task, whether that's a developer working with an API, an administrator configuring a system, or an end user learning a feature.
  • Treat documentation as part of the product experience and flag confusing behavior, UX issues, and defects discovered while documenting.
  • Use AI where it improves research, organization, editing, and consistency while keeping technical judgment and final authorship with the writer.
